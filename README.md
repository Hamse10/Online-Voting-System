"ONLINE VOTING SYSTEM" is an online voting technique. In this system, authorized voters can give their vote online without going to any physical polling station. A database is maintained to store all the names of voters with complete information. 
In the ONLINE VOTING SYSTEM, a voter can use their voting rights online without any difficulty. They must be registered first to vote. Registration is primarily done by the system administrator for security reasons. The system administrator registers voters on a special site accessible only to them by filling out a registration form. Citizens seeking registration are expected to contact the system administrator to submit their details and complete their registration. 
After registration, the voter is assigned a secret Voter ID, which they can use to log into the system and access the services provided, such as voting. If invalid or incorrect details are submitted, the citizen will not be registered to vote.
